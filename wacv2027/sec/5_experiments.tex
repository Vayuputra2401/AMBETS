\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}
BraTS 2024 glioma~\cite{menze2014brats,baid2021brats}: four co-registered MRI modalities, labels
remapped to $\{$0:Background, 1:NCR, 2:Edema, 3:ET$\}$, patient-wise $75/12.5/12.5$ split
($n_{\text{test}}{=}168$), $z$-scored per modality, $128^3$ inputs, $80$ epochs of AdamW
($10^{-4}$, cosine), mixed precision. The bottleneck is $192\times16^3$. Dice is reported two ways, labeled throughout: over
the nested BraTS regions---whole tumor (WT), tumor core (TC), enhancing tumor (ET)---and
\emph{per class} (NCR, edema, ET). The two differ slightly on ET, since the regions are nested and
the classes are not. We report a
SegResNet~\cite{myronenko2018segresnet} backbone throughout for comparability across
configurations; a Swin transformer with a UNETR decoder~\cite{hatamizadeh2021swinunetr}
reproduces every qualitative finding. All models are built on MONAI~\cite{cardoso2022monai}. Three configurations recur:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt
\item[] \textbf{NATURAL} --- residual expert, skips intact: the design a reader would build.
\item[] \textbf{GATED} --- subtractive repair: gated expert, $\alpha{=}0.5$.
\item[] \textbf{DIRECT} --- additive routing, Eq.~\ref{eq:direct}, $\lambda{=}1$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Provenance.}
We diagnosed the failure first on Swin and replicated it on SegResNet, which agree on every
qualitative finding (Sec.~\ref{sec:backbone2}). Every headline number, and all of GATED and DIRECT,
is SegResNet at $n{=}168$. Three NATURAL diagnostics ran on Swin only---the intervention pilot
($n{=}5$), expert divergence, and the linear probe---and carry a $\dagger$ wherever they appear.

\label{sec:aligns}

the concepts closely, and on concept-discriminability NATURAL reaches $0.88$ against $0.74$ for the
strongest post-hoc method, with integrated gradients and Grad-CAM near chance. By every measure
this literature reports, the module works.

Removing $\mathcal{L}_{\text{align}}$ collapses $\casfg$ to $-0.01/0.32/0.09$ while Dice barely

segmentation does not need the alignment.

\subsection{\ldots and is inert}
\label{sec:inert}

\paragraph{Intervention.}
Re-routing moves almost nothing. Averaged over concept pairs, $1.1\%$ of re-routed voxels change
label (Table~\ref{tab:repair}, GATED row; NATURAL gives $0.1\%$ on a smaller Swin pilot$^\dagger$). The
identity diagonal is exactly $0$, so this is a property of the model rather than of the
instrumentation.

\paragraph{The experts are not degenerate.}
The obvious explanation is false. Experts displace tokens by $0.59$--$0.74$ of their norm and
differ pairwise by $0.93$ (relative $L_2$, Swin$^\dagger$). The routing selects genuinely different
transformations; they simply do not reach the output.

\paragraph{Bottleneck ablation.}
They do not reach the output because the output does not need them. Replacing the module with raw
features or with \emph{zeros} costs almost nothing: on SegResNet the worst concept loses $3.1$
Dice (necrosis $0.514\!\to\!0.483$) and edema loses $0.6$; on Swin$^\dagger$, edema
$0.879\!\to\!0.865$ and ET $0.862\!\to\!0.806$. A decoder that can segment without its bottleneck will ignore any concept
structure placed there.

\subsection{The probe control}
\label{sec:probe}
A linear probe on the features entering the module scores $0.839$ against the router's
$0.872$ (Swin$^\dagger$).
Run on a model trained with \emph{no} concept supervision, the probe still reaches $\mathbf{0.811}$
---above every post-hoc method in Table~\ref{tab:aligns}. The concept structure is largely a
property of a competent segmentation encoder, not of the concept machinery.

\subsection{The output is a stronger concept classifier than the routing}
\label{sec:segpost}
A second control tightens the same point. The network's own segmentation posterior---free, from
the same forward pass---separates the concepts at $0.979$, well above the routing's $0.883$. It
reaches $0.982$ on the transformer backbone despite different pretraining and Dice, so the metric
saturates for any competent segmenter: under this window it nearly restates segmentation accuracy,
because ranking voxels is far easier than thresholding them. Matched to the router's $16^3$ decision budget by average-pooling and re-upsampling through
the identical operator, the posterior falls to $0.935$, so roughly half the residual gap is
spatial resolution rather than concept information.

We report this because omitting it would overstate our case. The posterior is not an explanation:
it \emph{is} the prediction, and restating the output explains nothing about how the output arose.
But with the probe control (Sec.~\ref{sec:probe}) it establishes that concept-discriminability
cannot carry an interpretability claim alone---an unsupervised linear probe clears the post-hoc
baselines, and the model's own output beats everything.

\paragraph{Subtractive repair fails, instructively.}
Attenuating the skips ($\alpha{=}0.5$) leaves every metric within noise of NATURAL: Dice $-0.002$ WT,
$\casfg$ $-0.001$ on edema, and causal control still at $0.011$. A scalar gate is compensable
during training---normalization affine parameters can simply rescale to undo it---so at
convergence it constrains nothing.

Removing the expert residual is worse than ineffective: pairwise divergence \emph{collapses} from
$0.93$ to $0.16$. With $E_k(h)=h+c_k(h)$ each expert supplies only a concept-specific correction
and is free to differ; with $E_k(h)=c_k(h)$ every expert must independently reconstruct whatever
the decoder needs, so they converge on nearly the same map. Making the expert the sole path made
the experts interchangeable.

\paragraph{Additive routing works.}
Table~\ref{tab:repair} gives the result. Causal control rises from $0.011$ $[0.009,0.014]$ to
$\mathbf{0.636}$ $[0.621,0.651]$, a paired difference of $+0.625$ $[+0.609,+0.641]$ with Wilcoxon
$p{=}2.6\times10^{-29}$ over $168$ matched cases. Fig.~\ref{fig:intervention} shows the same result
as images.

Two independent measurements corroborate it. Removing only the routing term collapses the model
(Table~\ref{tab:necessity}): NCR $-99\%$, ET $-80\%$, edema $-53\%$, against ${\le}6\%$ for
zeroing an inert bottleneck entirely. And $\rho$ converged at $0.35$, so the routing term
dominates the logits roughly $3\!:\!1$---the failure mode we instrumented for did not occur.

\paragraph{The price.}
Whole tumor is free ($0.927$ both); TC and ET cost ${\approx}1$ Dice point each.
Alignment is marginally \emph{better} than the inert baseline ($\casfg$ NCR $0.809$ vs.\ $0.802$),
calibration improves substantially (expected calibration error, ECE, $0.841\!\to\!0.681$), and token-level AUROC rises on edema
($0.638\!\to\!0.900$) and ET ($0.622\!\to\!0.839$) while falling on NCR ($0.937\!\to\!0.783$).

\paragraph{Alignment emerges from the task.}
One consequence deserves emphasis. Because the routing logits are part of the output,
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{seg}}$ itself trains the routing to be concept-aligned. By epoch 10, with
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{align}}$ still switched off, $\casfg$ has reached $0.41/0.42$ on edema and ET,
against $0.16/0.15$ for NATURAL. This directly answers the objection that $\casfg$ only
measures what $\mathcal{L}_{\text{align}}$ trains: here alignment appears without it.

\paragraph{The effect is specific and local.}
A large flip rate would mean little if the intervention perturbed everything, so we report two
controls separating concept-specific control from generic disturbance
(Table~\ref{tab:specificity}). Off-target classes move by $0.079$---an order of magnitude below
the $0.636$ target effect---and voxels \emph{outside} the re-routed region by $0.005$, while the
source class falls by $0.544$, mirroring the target's rise. The intervention does what it is
supposed to do, where it is supposed to do it.

\paragraph{Scope matters, and the smaller number is the honest one.}
A router assigns most of the volume to background---here roughly a quarter of all tokens go to
the edema expert, mostly normal tissue---so re-routing \emph{every} token of a concept repaints
the whole brain and reports $0.816$ rather than $0.636$. That figure is not wrong, but it mostly
shows that the output follows the routing everywhere, which under Eq.~\ref{eq:direct} is close to
tautological. Restricting to the tumor neighborhood measures concept control where it is
clinically meaningful, and halves the off-target disturbance while cutting out-of-region leakage
$3.5\times$ (Table~\ref{tab:specificity}). We quote the restricted number throughout and report
the scope with every measurement.

\paragraph{Where it is weakest, and why.}
The effect is not uniform, and the asymmetry is mechanistically informative rather than a mere
caveat. Necrotic core is weak as a \emph{source} ($0.28$--$0.31$) but works as a \emph{target}
($0.51$--$0.66$): the model can be pushed into calling a region necrosis more easily than pushed
off it. The source-class posteriors say why. Routing edema or ET tokens away drops their own class
by $0.81$ and $0.58$, whereas routing necrosis away drops it by $0.23$---the model was never
confident about necrosis to begin with (isolated Dice $0.483$, against $0.898$ for edema). There
is little confidence to remove, so the region is contested rather than reassigned and off-target
classes absorb the difference ($0.09$--$0.11$ against $0.03$--$0.06$ elsewhere). Causal control is
therefore bounded by how decisively the model held the original concept---a property of the
segmentation problem, not of the routing mechanism.

\subsection{Backbone transfer}
\label{sec:backbone2}
The natural design behaves the same on both backbones. A Swin transformer~\cite{liu2021swin,dosovitskiy2021vit} with a UNETR
decoder~\cite{hatamizadeh2022unetr}
reaches Dice $0.915/0.845/0.837$, $\casfg$ $0.69/0.94/0.93$ and discriminability $0.872$; the
SegResNet~\cite{myronenko2018segresnet} CNN reaches $0.927/0.883/0.869$, $0.80/0.96/0.95$ and $0.883$. They differ in
pretraining, capacity and accuracy, yet both align well and both are inert, so the leaks are
properties of the architecture family rather than of one backbone. The transformer shows the
placement failure of Sec.~\ref{sec:leaks} in its sharpest form: its module sits at stage~2 of~5,
so the two deeper, more semantic encoder stages bypass it entirely.
